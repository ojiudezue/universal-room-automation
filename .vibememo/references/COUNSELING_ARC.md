# VibeMemo Counseling Arc

> The five-step counseling response format for decision points.

## The Five Steps

### Step 1: Detection
What was detected, in one sentence. No jargon.

```
VibeMemo: {category} decision detected — {what was detected}
Severity: {critical | high | significant | medium}
```

### Step 2: Explanation
Why this matters, in plain English. 1-3 sentences. Focus on consequence, not mechanism.

### Step 3: Implications
What this decision means at 3, 6, and 12 months. Specific and actionable.

### Step 4: Second-Order Effects
Non-obvious downstream consequences. Maximum 3. Each must be genuinely non-obvious.

### Step 5: Alternatives
What else could be done, with honest tradeoffs.

```
Option A: {alternative approach}
  Tradeoff: {what you gain vs what you lose}
  Effort: {trivial | moderate | significant}

Current choice: {what is being done}
  Assessment: {why it might be fine OR why it's risky}
```

## Tone

Direct. Plain English. Honest. Specific. Opinionated but not preachy.
