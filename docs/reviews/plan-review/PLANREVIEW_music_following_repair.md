## Framing G: goal fidelity

| # | Sev | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| G-1 | **CRITICAL** | **The main use case is rejected by the confidence gate.** The plan treats confidence 0.6 as MF policy and calls BLE "solved". But the `confidence` MF gates on is not BLE confidence. It is the TransitionDetector's dwell-time heuristic. `duration` = time since the person ENTERED `from_room` (`transitions.py:288-301`; history is appended on each entry, `:383-400`). Over 60 s gives path `separate` and confidence **0.50** (`transitions.py:355-381`). Repro: Oji listens in the Office for 5 minutes, then walks to the Kitchen. duration=300, confidence 0.50 < 0.6, `low_confidence`, no transfer. Anyone who has been listening for more than a minute is filtered out. The audit's "1,039 eligible ≥0.6" are short-dwell pass-throughs. Today's `low_confidence 21` / `transfers_today 0` fits this. The operator's two successful tests were probably quick walks. Nothing in D0-D7 changes this, so the repaired payloads would almost never run. | `transitions.py:46, 288-381`; `music_following.py:521` |
| G-2 | HIGH | **Player-less rooms in between.** `from_room` is the immediately previous resolved room. A walk from A (music) through a hallway or room with no player to B produces A to hall (`to_player` None, skip) and then hall to B (`from_player` None, skip). Dense BLE resolves hallways as rooms, so this is likely common. The plan neither measures nor handles it. | `:811-826`; `transitions.py:168-190` |
| G-3 | MEDIUM | **"Keep the group" leaves music playing in the empty room.** That is not following. With C-3, the source sits at 10 percent indefinitely. §11 Q1 puts the decision to the operator, but D2 already hard-codes "keep" and deletes the unjoin knob. That is a risk reduction (avoiding the WiiM leader dissolve) that changes what is delivered. | §6 D2, §11 |
| G-4 | pass | No shadow, offer or probation stage. The feature ships ON, and logging is observability only. This matches the charter. | §0, §8 |
| G-5 | pass, but see G-1 | It does REUSE the dense-BLE per-person room producer (`person_coordinator.py:1059-1068`). The BLE distance gate (`:533-546`) is a real BLE input. The gating confidence is not. | |

## Required plan fixes (before build dispatch)
1. G-1: replace the gate. Use BLE-side confidence (person_coordinator `_calculate_confidence`) or gate on `path_type`, not dwell time. Alternatively, make MIN_CONFIDENCE ignore `separate` when the source is playing. Add a test: a 5-minute dwell followed by a walk transfers.
2. G-2: choose the source as "the person's most recent room whose player is playing, within N s" (new knob, with a rung), or prove from a D0-style probe that hallways do not resolve.
3. C-1, C-2, C-3: MA-only-if-MA-playing; chained-leader resolution; a fade and restore policy per path.
4. C-4 to C-8: name the missed sites.
