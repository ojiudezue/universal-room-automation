## Stated assumptions URA makes (referee these against your understanding)

### Assumption A1: "Net consumption" semantics

URA's `lifetime_net_energy_consumption` is read as **the cumulative kWh imported FROM the grid (net of any solar export)**. This is the same number your utility meter would read.

**Real-world equivalence:**
- Lifetime utility bill kWh ≈ value of `lifetime_net_energy_consumption` (in kWh; convert from MWh)
- Daily delta = today's grid-imported kWh
- Cost = daily delta × current_effective_rate

**Verify:** when Envoy IS available, `sensor.envoy_*_lifetime_net_energy_consumption` matches your utility's lifetime kWh on the bill (to within rounding).

**Risk:** Some Envoy installs report `lifetime_energy_consumption` as **gross household consumption** (including solar self-consumption) rather than NET (grid only). If URA is reading gross, it overcounts because it counts solar-self-consumed kWh as if they were imported from the grid.

### Assumption A2: "Production" is solar generation, period

URA's `lifetime_energy_production` represents the cumulative kWh from solar panels (including self-consumed AND exported). Daily delta is today's solar output.

**Risk:** Some installs split into separate CT readings (production-CT vs consumption-CT). If URA reads only one CT, daily production may be wrong.

### Assumption A3: "Energy import today" calculation

URA's `sensor.ura_energy_coordinator_energy_import_today = 36.505 kWh` represents:

```
energy_import_today = lifetime_net_consumption_now - lifetime_net_consumption_at_midnight_today
```

**This is the day's grid import.** Does NOT include self-consumed solar.

**Verify:** Compare URA's `energy_import_today` at end of day with your utility meter reading delta for the same day.

### Assumption A4: Cost calculation

```
cost_today = energy_import_today × current_effective_rate
           = energy_import_today × (TOU rate + delivery rate)
```

URA's TOU rate engine is fully PEC summer/winter aware. As of right now: $0.043 (TOU) + $0.042 (delivery) = $0.086/kWh.

**Verify:** Sample your utility bill for last month. Take total kWh × utility's stated rate per kWh. Compare to URA's `energy_cost_this_cycle` for that same period.

### Assumption A5: Predicted bill extrapolation

```
predicted_bill = energy_cost_this_cycle × (days_in_cycle / days_so_far)
```

Linear extrapolation from cycle-to-date. Doesn't account for:
- Seasonal HVAC ramp-up later in cycle
- TOU windows (assumes mix continues at same proportion)
- Solar pattern changes (e.g., extended cloudy stretch incoming)

**Risk:** Linear extrapolation is most accurate mid-cycle when the daily pattern is established. Early-cycle extrapolation is high-variance.

## Diagnosis options for the discrepancy

User: please tell me which direction the discrepancy is in:

1. **URA's number is HIGHER than utility's** (URA over-estimates consumption / under-counts solar offset).
2. **URA's number is LOWER than utility's** (URA under-estimates consumption / over-counts solar offset).

This determines which assumption is broken:

| Direction | Most likely cause |
|---|---|
| URA HIGHER (URA says you'll pay more than utility says) | A1 broken — URA is reading GROSS consumption (not net). Solar self-consumption is being counted as imported. The fix: switch URA's `lifetime_net_import_entity` config from `lifetime_energy_consumption` (gross) to `lifetime_net_energy_consumption` (net). |
| URA HIGHER (significant) | A4 broken — URA's `current_effective_rate` includes some fee that utility's published rate doesn't. Verify: is delivery_rate actually charged separately on your bill, or rolled into the energy rate? Some PEC plans have a "service availability charge" that's flat, not per-kWh. |
| URA LOWER | A3 broken — URA's daily delta is missing some hours (Envoy was offline for part of day, didn't accumulate). Check: did Envoy go unavailable mid-day yesterday? |
| URA LOWER (today especially) | Solar production reporting incorrectly subtracted twice. URA reads solar export, but if `lifetime_net_consumption` is ALREADY net-of-export at the meter, subtracting export again double-counts. Fix: use only ONE source (either lifetime_net_consumption directly, OR lifetime_consumption MINUS lifetime_production, never both). |
