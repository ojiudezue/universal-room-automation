## Verdict: **FIX-PLAN-FIRST**

The build groups (G1/G2) are well-scoped and the headline claims verify. But three G0 gates are mis-specified in ways that
**produce the wrong verdict on data that exists today** (Q1 would spuriously STOP G1; Q8 would spuriously GO the D5 half;
Q3/Q1 would bucket UTC hours as local). G0 is read-only, so the risk is wrong decisions, not damage. All fixes are plan
edits (probe spec), no redesign.
