## Findings

**MEDIUM-1: the Bermuda leg corroborates for one PersonCoordinator tick (<= 30 s), not 120 s, and only when the move comes before the face.**
Evidence:
- person_coordinator.py:1083 writes `previous_location = old_location` on every Bermuda tick, so `moved` (arrival_edges.py:63) is True only in the single tick the room changed. `UPDATE_INTERVAL` is 30 s (const.py:46).
- A face that comes before the Bermuda move is dropped at :823 and is never re-evaluated. Bermuda moves are not a release trigger.

Scenario: Oji (really away) walks in through the garage. The doorbell face fires at T, and Bermuda resolves garage at T+20 s. The gate says no, and nothing releases until GPS `not_home -> home` (P1).

Safety holds; this fails in the safe direction. But the README ("≤ 120 s from the face"; "the deciding corroborator is the Bermuda entry-area move") overstates the positive path: in practice face releases will be rare. The same narrowness was already in perimeter D10.5. The fix is either a doc correction or a design choice (for example a per-person Bermuda-entry stamp like `_arrival_edges`). The operator should decide. Class: #63-adjacent (the doc's window and the effective window differ).

**MEDIUM-2: the shared boot-exclusion and window constants couple an unlock-safety budget to a perimeter de-escalation budget. The boot exclusion is load-bearing for locks.**
After an HA restart the first PersonCoordinator tick has `old_location = "unknown"` (person_coordinator.py:973). That makes `moved` True, and `last_changed` is the restore time. Meanwhile `_really_away` returns True for the whole boot-arrival window (:958).

Repro if someone retunes `PERIMETER_EGRESS_ARRIVAL_BOOT_EXCLUDE_S` to 0 (for example to make perimeter de-escalation work right after a restart):
- the garage lockout is engaged (SLEEP);
- HA restarts;
- the person's Bermuda area sensor restores to an entry area;
- a mislabelled face for that person arrives within 120 s;
- result: unlock.

The README's "one rule" ruling is the operator's, but these two consumers carry opposite risk directions: a DE-ESCALATE versus an UNLOCK. Recommendation: put a comment on both constants in const.py saying the lock regime depends on BOOT_EXCLUDE_S ≥ the boot-arrival window, or add a lock-side floor `max(BOOT_EXCLUDE_S, GARAGE_LOCKOUT_BOOT_ARRIVAL_WINDOW_S)`. This is a knob-extreme leak (rung-1, so a change needs review), not a default-config leak. Class: #63 (coincidental equality hides a concept split).

**MEDIUM-3 (UNVERIFIED, needs a probe): a Bermuda area flap could manufacture a fresh "move" for an absent person.**
If Bermuda keeps an absent device's last area, then a Bermuda reload or flap does this: the area goes `unavailable`, PersonCoordinator drops to the person_state branch (location = away), and the area returns as `garage` with a fresh `last_changed`. The next tick then reads as a move into an entry area inside the window, outside the boot exclusion.

I have not verified what Bermuda's area sensor reports for an unseen device. I'd be guessing. The perimeter has the same exposure. Probe: recorder history of `sensor.<oji>_area` (Bermuda) during a known trip (10-24) and across a Bermuda reload. If it holds a room name while the person is away, add `old_location not in (away/not_home/unknown)`-style discharge, or require `last_bermuda_update` freshness.

**LOW-1: the lock-side entry areas are the UNION of every door group's interior neighbours** (arrival_edges.py:131-133).
The perimeter uses only the triggering camera's group (perimeter_alert.py:4989-4998). So for locks, a same-person Bermuda hop into any neighbour room (for example the kitchen) counts as an arrival.
- `_really_away` (≥ 15 min tracker `not_home`) still bounds it. A leak needs the person's own phone in the house while GPS reads away for 15 min or more, plus a mislabelled face of that person inside the tick window.
- Neighbour entries that are stems rather than entity_ids resolve to "" and are skipped silently (fail-safe).
- Recommendation: restrict to egress-camera areas plus configured neighbours. This is a design choice, so record it in the README.

**INFO-1 (out of the invariant's scope, pre-existing): house-state releases are not identity-gated.**
- `on_house_state_changed` releases on `arriving` / `home_*` / `waking` (:748-757).
- Census faces feed house state. The face of a resident who reads raw `not_home` is dropped by the stale-face latch guard (presence doc §1, CENSUS-BLE-OVER-FACE-1), so the 10-24 shape can't exit GUEST or VACATION through census. Inference produces ARRIVING only from AWAY (presence.py:1367), and AWAY is inert for lockouts (:768).

No leak was found, but this path is outside what the cycle gated.
