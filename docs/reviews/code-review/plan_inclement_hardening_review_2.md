## Decision-justification checks

- **Blind floor default 60.** The reasoning (do not pin reserve at 100 overnight and lose self-consumption) is sound, but the value has no evidence behind it: no overnight-load-versus-capacity number. It is also unsafe as specified (P2-03, P2-04). It is acceptable as a rung-3 knob once it becomes `max(reserve, partial_floor, knob)`.
- **Forecast as corroboration.** Defensible as a policy, but it does not deliver tonight's case under defaults (P2-02). The hourly-field and provider-count assumptions are unverified. Probe before building D2.
