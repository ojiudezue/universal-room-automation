**⚠️ A-LOW-1 — obs-mode stale freeze latch.** `_update_freeze_active()` is called ONLY at `hvac.py:1479`, inside the `if not self._observation_mode:` block (`:917`). But `_predictor.update()` (`:945`) runs UNGATED by obs mode, and the predictor's `_check_pre_conditioning` can emit setpoints (pre-heat `hvac_predict.py:1061`). In observation mode the latch never refreshes, so a predictor emit reads a stale / init-`False` `_freeze_active` and would skip the floor during a real freeze. **Severity LOW:** obs mode is non-actuating by intent and the floor is a safety net (normal presets hold ≥58°F); the predictor emitting in obs mode is a pre-existing behavior, not introduced here. Optional hardening: call `_update_freeze_active()` once unconditionally at the top of `_run_decision_cycle`, OR have the predictor refresh via its HC ref.
