## Checklist (a)-(h)

- **(a) Emission paths:** the plan misses `force_redispatch` (R1-04), misclassifies `:5952` (R1-05), and misses the narration caller `:6687` (R1-06). The blind write at `:5664-5673`, the EVSE append at `energy.py:5516` and the in-place update are correctly identified. Pool `number.set_value` (`energy.py:8710/8734`, `energy_pool.py:411/429`) targets pool speed and is out of scope; the lint allowlist must show it is a different target.
- **(b) Floors:** the registry invents 4 floors and mis-values the only real one (R1-01, R1-02). The DP floor is overlay-only (R1-10). Branch-local `max(soc-5, eff)` and the EVSE/DP max stay where they are. Correct.
- **(c) Consumers:** `_last_reserve_level_desired` readers are `energy_write_verify.py:1027, :1720`, `energy.py:5469`, `force_redispatch :6790`. `_last_reserve_level` readers are `energy_battery.py:2429`, `energy.py:2018/2318`, write-verify `:1055`. Under a single `partial_hold` registry these values are unchanged. Under the registry as written they change (R1-01). A history attribute changes no consumer, but it puts the recorder at risk (R1-08). The reason text changes (R1-06).
- **(d) INV-G guard:** none (R1-07). Needs an AST chokepoint lint.
- **(e) Restart / recorder:** the sensor isn't a RestoreEntity; persistence is the DB `wv_commanded_ledger` (R1-03). The attribute must be unrecorded (R1-08).
- **(f) Scope creep:** the extra 5 registry floors are scope creep and behaviour change. Cut them. The minimal slice is one registered floor, the history, and the chokepoint lint. Restart persistence of the history is optional; it is in the operator's ask, so keep it, on the DB key.
- **(g) Prior art:** the claim is false (R1-12, HIGH). Reuse the lint plus durable `climate_write`-style rows. Card `WriteResult` for the dispatch tap. Reject leases for floors. The energy `command_trail` was also missed (R1-11).
- **(h) Knobs / acceptance:** the knob rung is fine; recompute the maxlen rationale. The "8h" sizing is wrong (R1-08). The byte-identical snapshot criterion is untestable as written under `partial_hold` (R1-06). Fix the Live entity_id (R1-03). Replace the soak criterion (R1-15).
