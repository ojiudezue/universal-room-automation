active stuck flag (`_stuck_sensor_kinds[s] in {"continuous","dutycycle"}`) AND
there exists at least one entity `c` in the same room's Tier-1 CONF lists with
`resolve_role(room_config, c, RoleQuery.CORROBORATOR_FOR_ROOM) is True` AND that

`s` MUST NOT contribute to `motion_detected` / `presence_detected` /
`occupancy_detected` at ANY tier (room substrate, zone tracker, house infer)
for this tick.

either (a) the house is in `{SLEEP, WAKING, HOME_NIGHT}`, OR (b) no
CORROBORATOR_FOR_ROOM entity is wired in that room's Tier-1 CONF lists, OR (c)
the wired corroborator agrees (is ON or was last-ON within
`CORROBORATOR_DISAGREE_S`), the behaviour of `s` MUST be byte-identical to
today: the flag fires an NM per existing dedup, but the sensor still
contributes to occupancy. **Notify-only stays** for rooms without a
corroborator — the operator has been told this, and rec-1 hardware in
