- DASH-V8: would be REOPEN if the card were absent from the dashboard or the helpers had been deleted without a replacement; both counter-observations checked (card present, helpers present). Producer/consumer: the card reads `weather.phalanxmadrone` and the two template helpers, which read the `house_avg_*` attributes of the URA delta-outside sensors; nothing else reads the helpers (not re-verified repo-wide; the card's own `next` is the only claim).

- WA-BRIDGE: `delivered=1` alone cannot disprove a silent failure (no ack, `notification_manager.py:2642`); the card's own earlier PASS (post-restart 11-08 send arrives, README_v5.106.1 Validated) is the delivery proof, the 58 later rows show the path keeps being taken. Falsifier = any undelivered whatsapp row or a bridge 401 log, none seen (log read unavailable).
- GUEST-MODE-SPLIT: falsifier for "stay rules work" would be a guest row without an `entry_qualified` night or a guest row after 2 empty nights. The only guest rows (11-14) follow the 11-13 qualifying night. Note the day-after: no re-entry on 11-15 even though the guest may still have been home is the rule's designed behaviour (basis 11-14 = 0.058), not verified against ground truth that the guest actually left (operator knowledge needed).
- LOCK-ENTRY: zero `egress_identity` rows in 2 days of persistence is a fire-check finding in itself: either no dual-role camera alert happened (plausible, house quiet) or the gate is not reached. Cannot discriminate without an event. `PERIMETER-GATE-DECISIONS-NOT-PERSISTED-1` (v5.108.20) made this measurable only from 11-14.
